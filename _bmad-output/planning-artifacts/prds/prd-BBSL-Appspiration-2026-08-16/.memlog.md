- (decision) OQ-2 RESOLVED (was blocking): Minor League Slot cap evasion is ALLOWED. A won eligible player stashed in minors carries a $0 cap hit regardless of winning amount; the constraint is that the team cannot activate him without absorbing the full amount, enforced in Fantrax in-season, not in this app
- (change) FR-35 rewritten from conservative full commitment to unbounded-with-overflow. Maximum Bid is now PER-AUCTION not per-team: unbounded on an eligible player a free minors slot can absorb, ordinary formula otherwise. New glossary terms: Free Minor League Slots (M), Eligible Leading Bids (N), Overflow Count = max(0,N-M), Minors Exposure = sum of the Overflow Count LARGEST eligible leading bids, Projected Active/Bench Additions
- (decision) Minors Exposure sized on the LARGEST surplus bids because Slot Placement follows close order and a bidder cannot steer which win lands in minors. Consequence: a later cheap eligible bid can be refused by exposure from an earlier expensive one; the app refuses the NEW bid and never retroactively voids an accepted one
- (change) Knock-on edits: Roster Reserve formula generalized (Leading Bid Count -> Projected Active/Bench Additions, reduces identically for the all-non-eligible case), FR-12/FR-13/FR-21/FR-24/FR-25 updated, new Non-Goal 'not enforcing activation', 2 risks rewritten, examples 18-20 added, OQ-2 removed and OQ-3..OQ-8 renumbered to OQ-2..OQ-6, addendum concurrency race #4 added
- (event) Update run opened, driven by bmad-architecture spine ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md (architecture-BBSL-Appspiration-2026-08-16). Four amendments: two blocking rule contradictions found by the spine's adversarial and rules-correctness reviewer lenses, plus two forced by a zero-budget constraint.
- (decision) BLOCKING #1 RESOLVED: §10 examples 18-20 contradicted the §3 Glossary (example 19 used M=1 where 'M = 3 minus currently occupied' gives M=3). Diagnosed as a slip introduced when examples 18-20 were added during the FR-35 unbounded-with-overflow rewrite, not a deliberate rule. The Glossary rule was tested and is sound. FIX: keep the rule unchanged; change the examples' setup so Team P holds TWO occupied Minor League Slots (M=1), which is the only value making all three examples cohere. Example 20's lesson shifts from 'exposure returns to $0' to 'exposure drops from $30M to $1M' - the sharper point being that an OPEN leading bid creates exposure while a CLOSED one does not, because it is now a $0 cap hit in a slot.
- (decision) BLOCKING #2 RESOLVED: a Minimum-Bid Contention on a Minor League Eligible player was governed by two contradictory rules - the §3 Glossary and FR-14 committed a flat $1,000,000 per Contender, while FR-35 committed only Minors Exposure. Traced to a stale assumption logged BEFORE the FR-35 rewrite and never revisited when that rewrite superseded it. FIX: FR-35 wins. A $1M contention on an eligible Player counts as an Eligible Leading Bid of $1,000,000 feeding Minors Exposure, committing nothing while a Free Minor League Slot exists. Rationale: a lottery win on an eligible player lands in a slot at a $0 Cap Hit, so holding $1M against the cap for him is simply wrong. Flat $1M is retained unchanged for contentions on NON-eligible Players.
- (change) FR-4 changed from magic-link email to Discord OAuth. Forced by a zero-budget constraint: no free email tier meets the required burst without a deliverability risk that would break SM-3 invisibly (Brevo 300/day but tests badly into Outlook/Hotmail and Yahoo; Mailjet ~200/day; SMTP2GO 1000/month). The PRD had already tagged magic-link as an [ASSUMPTION] acknowledging 'an equally reasonable substitute'. Discord OAuth is also strictly better here: one tap on a phone already signed into Discord, and it yields the Discord user id that FR-27's mentions need - authentication and the notification mapping become the same fact.
- (change) FR-27 changed from email notifications to Discord @mentions in the league channel. The FR's own job-to-be-done is 'tell me the moment I'm outbid, on the channel I actually read', which for this league is Discord. The three mutable categories become mention-or-no-mention: the event is posted either way so the public record stays complete, only the ping is suppressed. Outbid and Contract Assignment Phase notices remain non-mutable. Supersedes the earlier decision 'Notifications v1 = email + Discord webhook'.
- (change) NEW RISK to record: Discord now carries BOTH authentication and notification, so a Discord outage is total - nobody can sign in and nobody is alerted, simultaneously. Spine AD-27 mandates three mitigations: >=30 day sessions, a commissioner break-glass sign-in independent of Discord so FR-34 pause stays reachable, and a liveness alert that does not route through Discord. Also a new precondition: all 31 managers must have Discord accounts, now load-bearing for ACCESS not just convenience.
- (change) §5 concurrency NFR: 'Bid acceptance must be serialized per Auction' is superseded by the spine's AD-6 global serialization. Per-auction locking is provably insufficient because Maximum Bid depends on the bidding Team's leading positions in OTHER auctions (Committed Bids, Minors Exposure), so two co-managers bidding simultaneously on two different auctions each pass their own per-auction check and jointly breach the cap.
