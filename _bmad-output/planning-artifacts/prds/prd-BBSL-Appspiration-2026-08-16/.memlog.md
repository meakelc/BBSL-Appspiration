- (change) FR-35 rewritten from conservative full commitment to unbounded-with-overflow. New glossary terms: Free Minor League Slots (M), Eligible Leading Bids (N), Overflow Count = max(0,N-M), Minors Exposure = sum of the Overflow Count LARGEST eligible leading bids, Projected Active/Bench Additions
- (decision) Minors Exposure sized on the LARGEST surplus bids because Slot Placement follows close order and a bidder cannot steer which win lands in minors. Consequence: a later cheap eligible bid can be refused by exposure from an earlier expensive one; the app refuses the NEW bid and never retroactively voids an accepted one

Diagnosed as a slip introduced when examples 18-20 were added during the FR-35 unbounded-with-overflow rewrite, not a deliberate rule. The Glossary rule was tested and is sound. FIX: keep the rule unchanged; change the examples' setup so Team P holds TWO occupied Minor League Slots (M=1), which is the only value making all three examples cohere. Example 20's lesson shifts from 'exposure returns to $0' to 'exposure drops from $30M to $1M' - the sharper point being that an OPEN leading bid creates exposure while a CLOSED one does not, because it is now a $0 cap hit in a slot.
- (decision) BLOCKING #2 RESOLVED: a Minimum-Bid Contention on a Minor League Eligible player was governed by two contradictory rules - the §3 Glossary and FR-14 committed a flat $1,000,000 per Contender, while FR-35 committed only Minors Exposure. Traced to a stale assumption logged BEFORE the FR-35 rewrite and never revisited when that rewrite superseded it. FIX: FR-35 wins. A $1M contention on an eligible Player counts as an Eligible Leading Bid of $1,000,000 feeding Minors Exposure, committing nothing while a Free Minor League Slot exists. Rationale: a lottery win on an eligible player lands in a slot at a $0 Cap Hit, so holding $1M against the cap for him is simply wrong. Flat $1M is retained unchanged for contentions on NON-eligible Players.
- (change) FR-4 changed from magic-link email to Discord OAuth. Forced by a zero-budget constraint: no free email tier meets the required burst without a deliverability risk that would break SM-3 invisibly (Brevo 300/day but tests badly into Outlook/Hotmail and Yahoo; Mailjet ~200/day; SMTP2GO 1000/month). The PRD had already tagged magic-link as an [ASSUMPTION] acknowledging 'an equally reasonable substitute'. Discord OAuth is also strictly better here: one tap on a phone already signed into Discord, and it yields the Discord user id that FR-27's mentions need - authentication and the notification mapping become the same fact.
- (change) FR-27 changed from email notifications to Discord @mentions in the league channel. The FR's own job-to-be-done is 'tell me the moment I'm outbid, on the channel I actually read', which for this league is Discord. - (assumption) The +1 Outstanding Bid Allowance carries a precondition of at least one Free Active/Bench Slot. Read as a flat ceiling of 13 the rule breaks the invariant FR-37 exists to protect: a Team at Roster Count 12 holding one bid simply wins it, because FR-40 cancellation only fires when ANOTHER close fills the roster and a Team holding one bid has no other close -- landing on Roster Count 13, which FR-30 then refuses to export. The precondition is the only reading under which the hard ceiling survives, and it matches the precondition the commissioner stated explicitly for lotteries in the same sentence. - (decision) FR-40 cancellation trigger is any Auction Close that reduces the winning Team's free slots -- Active/Bench OR Minor League -- not one that increases Roster Count. A Team at Roster Count 12 with one free minors slot may hold eligible lottery entries under FR-18; winning one keeps Roster Count at 12 but drops M to 0, leaving the rest with nowhere to land. A roster-count trigger would not fire, both remaining lotteries could be won, and the Team would finish at Roster Count 14. Section 10 example 35 is that case.
- (assumption) An Auction restored to Awaiting Opening Bid has its Auction Clock CLEARED, not left running. The approved proposal said a cancellation leaves the clock unaltered; applied literally to an auction with no surviving bid that leaves a clock running on an auction nobody is in, closing with no winner at an arbitrary hour -- a state FR-21 does not define. Clearing it makes the auction identical to an unbid nomination, which the league already ruled on (the FR-9 rules-as-written decision, section 10 example 12): it holds its nominator's slot until someone opens it or the phase ends. 'Clock unchanged' governs the case where a bid survives, which is the case it was stated about. Section 10 example 33.
- (decision) Overflow Count split into TWO named glossary terms rather than one figure with a parameter. Overflow Count (money side) keeps counting lottery entries and feeds Minors Exposure; new term Active/Bench Overflow (slots side) excludes them and is the only overflow reaching Projected Active/Bench Additions. Forced by FR-18 exempting contentions from roster capacity but not from the cap. They were one figure from 2026-08-17 to 2026-09-08 and are one subtraction apart, so compute-once-use-twice is now wrong in a way no single test announces -- it over-counts capacity for a Team in lotteries and refuses bids the league allows. Logged as a section 9 risk.
- (decision) CONFLICT with a prior recorded decision, resolved by narrowing not reversal: the 2026-08-17 FR-35 decision that 'the app refuses the NEW bid and never retroactively voids an accepted one' is now false as blanket wording, because FR-40 cancels accepted bids. Narrowed to its actual domain -- no ARITHMETIC ever reaches back and invalidates an accepted bid. FR-40 is not an exception: it is not triggered by arithmetic and does not find the bid invalid; it stands a valid bid down because a slot it needed was filled, a change in the world rather than a correction of the record. Applied to FR-35's consequence bullet and the section 9 overflow risk mitigation.

- (decision) A Roster Move or Drop that would leave either Team failing a gate is REFUSED outright, never cascaded around. Rejected the alternative of reusing FR-40's restorer to cancel surplus bids: it would require amending AD-31, whose trigger rule ('cancellation is caused by an Auction Close and by nothing else') was deliberately bounded to stop cascade re-entrancy, and it would let an administrative act strip a Player from a Team that took no action. The Commissioner's remedy is FR-32's void or waiting for a Close -- both already exist and both already record themselves.
- (decision) THREE LEAGUE RULES CONFIRMED by the commissioner 2026-09-10, same day they were raised, so they enter as settled rules rather than tagged assumptions. (1) The second-round rookie term is 5 years and INVARIANT across every 2RK deal -- that invariance is what makes 'full term unelapsed' a sound test for 'drafted this year' and removes any need for a per-offseason constant. (2) Dropping a Minor League Slot player yields NO Dead Money: the governing rule is that a Drop converts the Player's CHARGED Cap Hit into Dead Money at the same amount, and a stashed Player charges 0 by placement. This collapsed three special cases into one rule and produced the counter-example to 'a Drop lowers Maximum Bid' -- a stashed drop raises it, via M rising and Minors Exposure recomputing. The initially-specified v1 refusal was withdrawn. (3) Unknown arrivals cannot legitimately occur, since every acquisition in the window goes through the auction; reported as an ERROR rather than as a state needing a mechanism.
- (decision) Dead Money is a fourth RosterSlotKind rather than a parallel concept. The two axes -- does it charge, does it count toward 12 -- are already independent in the core: chargedCapHit returns 0 only for minor_league, and Roster Count counts only active_bench. Dead Money is therefore structurally Injury Reserve without the ceiling of 2, and chargedCapHit already returns the right value for it by falling through. One enum member, one unbounded SLOT_CEILINGS entry, one migration on team_rosters_roster_slot_kind_check, one regex capture group in the Fantrax adapter. A parallel dead-money table would have been a second definition of Cap Space.
- (assumption) A Roster Move is NOT broadcast to Discord (commissioner's choice, audit-log-only). This makes it the one Commissioner act the league is not told about, in a product whose thesis is that nobody should have to wonder whether the app got it right. Accepted cost: a Manager sees a rival's Cap Space and Maximum Bid change with no announcement and must open the Audit Log to learn why. The only live inference in this amendment.
