- (decision) CAP-19 (Roster Capacity) AMENDED, id preserved. Its rule is no longer 'Roster Count + Projected Active/Bench Additions <= 12'. A Bid now passes when Projected Active/Bench Additions = 0, OR the Team holds at least one Free Active/Bench Slot and Projected <= Free + 1 -- the Outstanding Bid Allowance, which exists so a Manager can chase two Players with one open slot rather than idling a day for a close. The 12-slot ceiling is unchanged; the allowance is a bidding permission, never a thirteenth Slot. The free-slot PRECONDITION is load-bearing: without it a Team at Roster Count 12 holds one bid, wins it, and lands on 13 with no other close available to cancel it.
- (capability) CAP-21 ADDED (next unused id; CAP-1..CAP-20 preserved, none renumbered) -- Cancel a surplus commitment and restore the Auction. Realizes FR-40. Intent: when an Auction Close leaves a Team holding commitments with no Roster Slot to receive them, the system stands them down and returns each affected Auction to its next-highest bidder. This is the other half of CAP-19's allowance and is not separately shippable: the allowance without the cascade lets a Team win a thirteenth Player.
- (decision) CAP-8 (Minimum-Bid Contention) AMENDED: entries leave the roster-capacity gate entirely. A Team with at least one Free Active/Bench Slot -- or, for an eligible Player, a Free Minor League Slot -- may be a Contender in ANY number of open Contentions; entries contribute nothing to Projected Active/Bench Additions and do not consume the allowance. Cap space is the only quantitative limit. Rationale from the commissioner: the expected outcome of a lottery is losing, so a capacity rule sized as though a Team would win them all throttles the mechanism that clears cheap Players. Safe only because CAP-21's cascade removes a Team from its remaining lotteries before they draw, which AD-11's sequential closing guarantees. Also: the recorded Contender list is the post-cancellation list, and an empty list closes with no winner.
- (constraint) TWO OVERFLOW FIGURES, NOT ONE. Overflow Count (money side) counts Contention entries and feeds Minors Exposure; Active/Bench Overflow (slots side) excludes them and is the only overflow reaching Projected Active/Bench Additions. Forced by CAP-8's exemption: entries leave the capacity gate but stay in Committed Bids. They were one figure from 2026-08-17 to 2026-09-08 and are one subtraction apart, so the natural implementation -- compute once, use twice -- is now wrong in a way no single test announces, over-counting capacity for a Team in lotteries and refusing bids the league allows.
- (constraint) ONE RESTORER, TWO CALLERS (AD-31, new). A cancellation appends one BidCancelled compensating event carrying its own decision -- cancelled seq, cause, and either the restored Team/seq/amount or null -- and the projection READS that rather than re-deriving it by re-running gates inside a fold. close.ts is the sole appender: AuctionClosed first, then each BidCancelled in cascade order; restore.ts decides and appends nothing; restoration is evaluated against post-close state. The selection algorithm lives in ONE pure function consumed by both FR-40's cancellation and CAP-14's Commissioner void, which differ only in three parameters (retain-vs-erase in the fold, leave-vs-restore the Auction Clock, keep-vs-remove the League Clock reset). No migration: event_type is a generic text column.
